A Path to
Lasting Change

At Brooklyn Meditation, we go beyond temporary calm — we help you remove the root cause of cluttered, unnecessary thoughts. Through a step-by-step process of self-reflection and letting go, you can thoroughly cleanse your mind and experience lasting happiness.

We offer live guided meditation classes, available both in-person in Park Slope and online 7 days a week, making it easy to stay consistent and supported, wherever you are.

About the Practice

A guided method that goes deeper than relaxation

Most meditation teaches you to manage thoughts. Our method goes further — it teaches you to release them. By systematically discarding the accumulated images, habits, and stories of your past, you uncover the true mind that was always there.

The practice is step-by-step, guided, and accessible to everyone — whether you've meditated before or are starting fresh.
