Welcome

A quiet place to come back to yourself

Brooklyn Meditation opened in Park Slope in 2015 with a simple mission: to offer a genuine, guided path to inner peace. Our method is not relaxation — it's a step-by-step practice of releasing the mind's accumulated clutter to reveal the stillness that's always been within you.

About the Practice

A guided method that goes deeper than relaxation

Most meditation teaches you to manage thoughts. Our method goes further — it teaches you to release them. By systematically discarding the accumulated images, habits, and stories of your past, you uncover the true mind that was always there.

The practice is step-by-step, guided, and accessible to everyone — whether you've meditated before or are starting fresh.
