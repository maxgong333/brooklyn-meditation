Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Brooklyn Meditation ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit brooklynmeditation.nyc, book a session, or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, email address, phone number, and any message you send when you book an intro session, fill out a form (including Google ad lead forms), use our website chat, call, or email us.
  • Booking and payment information. Class and appointment bookings, memberships, and payments are handled by our booking provider, WellnessLiving. Payment card details are processed by WellnessLiving and its payment processors; we do not store card numbers.
  • Usage information. When you visit our website, we and our service providers automatically collect information such as pages viewed, links clicked, device and browser type, approximate location, and the ad or website that referred you. We collect this with cookies and similar technologies through Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, and Google Ads.

How we use your information

  • To schedule and confirm your sessions and respond to your questions.
  • To send you information about classes, events, and our community. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand how visitors use our website and to measure and improve our advertising.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our programs, such as WellnessLiving (booking and payments), Google (analytics and advertising), and Crisp (website chat), and only as needed for them to provide their services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google Ads conversion tracking and Google Analytics to learn which ads and pages lead people to book with us. You can control cookies in your browser settings, manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com, and opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you emails, by contacting us at the address below.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
