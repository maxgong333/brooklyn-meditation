Our Approach

Simple, Powerful &
Systematic

A step-by-step guided meditation method designed for anyone — regardless of experience — to discover lasting peace within.

The Method

Meditation that goes to the root

Most relaxation techniques help you feel calm in the moment. Our method is different: it teaches you to permanently release the mental images, habits, and stories that cause stress, anxiety, and suffering.

The practice is drawn from a deep Korean meditation tradition and adapted for everyday modern life. Through guided visualization and reflection, you systematically discard the accumulated "false mind" — returning to the original, peaceful nature you've always had.

The result isn't temporary calm. It's a fundamental shift in how you experience yourself and the world.

How It Works

A step-by-step path

The meditation unfolds in progressive levels — each building on the last — so you always know exactly where you are and where you're going.

01

Recall

Bring to mind the memories, emotions, and experiences that have shaped your sense of self — clearly and without judgment.

02

Reflect

See these mental images for what they are: pictures in the mind, not reality. Observe the gap between "you" and your thoughts.

03

Release

Let go of each image completely. As you discard the clutter, the original still, peaceful mind naturally reveals itself.

04

Return

Rest in the silence of the true mind — spacious, clear, and undisturbed. This is your original nature.

The Founder

Woo Myung & the Origin of the Method

The meditation method practiced at Brooklyn Meditation was developed by Woo Myung, a Korean author, poet, and spiritual teacher who himself attained enlightenment through rigorous practice.

After his own awakening in the mid-1990s, Woo Myung devoted himself to developing a systematic method that could guide anyone — regardless of background — to the same realization: that the true mind is infinite, peaceful, and always present.

Today, centers practicing this method exist in over 40 countries, each teaching the same step-by-step approach that has helped hundreds of thousands of people find lasting peace.
